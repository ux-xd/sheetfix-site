Cold Email Rescue

Your cold email is landing in spam, or your domain reputation is shot, and nobody at the ESP can tell you why. A written audit that tells you exactly what is broken, fixed price, no call required.

What's wrong

  • Google, Yahoo and Microsoft now enforce bulk-sender rules. Spam complaints under 0.3%, bounces under 2%, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC all required. Miss one and inbox placement collapses across every campaign you send.
  • Recovery is slow on your own. After a spam trap hits, expect about 21 days to claw back Google and closer to 47 for Microsoft, if the underlying problem is actually fixed.
  • Most ESP dashboards hide the cause. They show you open rates. They do not show you the missing DMARC record, the stale include in your SPF, or the shared IP that got you blocklisted.

What you get

A written audit, delivered as a document, that covers:

  • Authentication validation. SPF parsed record by record, DKIM verified against your actual sending selectors, DMARC policy and reporting checked.
  • Blocklist checks. Spamhaus, Barracuda, SpamCop and Microsoft SNDS guidance for your sending IPs, with delisting steps if anything is listed.
  • Sending-practice review. Your volume, list hygiene and complaint exposure measured against the 2025-2026 bulk-sender requirements.
  • A prioritized remediation list. Numbered fixes in the order that recovers inbox placement fastest, each with the exact record or setting to change.
